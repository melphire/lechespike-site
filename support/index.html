01

Getting started

How do I take my first shot?+

Allow Camera access when prompted, choose a look along the bottom of the viewfinder, and tap the shutter. Allow Photos access to save the result. Tap the thumbnail to open your captures in Apple Photos. Microphone access is used only when recording sound with video.

What can I use for free?+

The Leche and Tinta looks and the 28 Clásico lens are free. LECHE Pro adds the other looks, lens characters and premium tools. Features marked Pro require an active subscription; hardware-dependent options also need a compatible iPhone.

Can I change the photo shape?+

Open Leche Spike Settings → Photo → Aspect to choose full-screen 9:16 or classic 3:4. Available resolution and format choices depend on your iPhone. The app explains when a full-screen crop changes the saved megapixel count.

02

Camera & photos

The camera or microphone isn’t available.+

Open iPhone Settings → Apps → LECHE and check Camera and Microphone access. Microphone permission is used for sound when recording video. Return to the app and try again. If LECHE says the camera is paused, bring the app to the foreground and tap “Unpause camera.”

A photo or video won’t save.+

Check Photos access in iPhone Settings → Apps → LECHE, and check that the phone has free storage. Until it can be saved, LECHE keeps the shot and lists it in LECHE Settings → Unsaved photos and videos. If saving still fails, email the exact message and your format setting, such as HEIF or JPEG. Keep the app open while a save is finishing.

Do all iPhone models support every option?+

No. Available resolutions, RAW formats, virtual lens effects and depth features depend on the camera hardware. Some features also require a paid subscription. The app shows available choices for your device. Leche Spike is designed for iPhone, with iOS 26 or later.

Where do my photos and videos go?+

Straight to your iPhone Photos library. Tap the gallery button in the camera to open Photos. With full Photos access, each photo keeps its unedited original, so you can revert it in Photos.

03

Your LECHE Pro membership

What does LECHE Pro include, and what does it cost?+

LECHE Pro unlocks all twelve looks, lens character, manual controls, RAW and 4K video on supported iPhones. The annual subscription has a US storefront price of US$39.99 per year, with Apple-localized prices elsewhere. Eligible customers see a 3-day free trial only when the App Store offers it; check the price and renewal terms before confirming. See our subscription terms.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?+

In LECHE, open Settings and tap Manage subscription. You can also open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Deleting LECHE doesn’t cancel a subscription.

I subscribed, but LECHE still shows the free version.+

Open LECHE Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you used to subscribe. This also restores access on a new or reset iPhone. If a purchase is waiting for approval, for example through Ask to Buy, access updates once it’s approved.

How do I ask for a refund?+

Apple handles App Store billing and refunds. Request a refund through Apple’s Report a Problem page.
