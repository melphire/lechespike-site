Privacy policy

This website does not accept payments or start a subscription.

App license

When downloaded from the App Store, LECHE is licensed under Apple’s standard End User License Agreement. Your App Store use and payments are also subject to Apple’s applicable terms. Your statutory consumer rights remain unaffected.

Annual paid access

The paid option is LECHE Pro, an auto-renewable annual subscription to LECHE’s premium features. The app shows the included features, subscription period and localized App Store price before you confirm payment. Features also depend on your iPhone’s hardware and the available app version.

Access to subscription features continues while your annual subscription is active, subject to the applicable App Store terms.

Price and introductory trial

The US storefront price is US$39.99 per year. Other storefronts use Apple-localized prices. The actual price and any available introductory offer are displayed in the app and Apple’s confirmation before you subscribe.

Where offered by the App Store, eligible customers can start with a 3-day free trial. If you confirm that offer, it converts automatically to the annual subscription at the displayed price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Trial eligibility is determined by Apple and is not guaranteed for previous subscribers. If no eligible trial is shown, the annual subscription starts at the displayed price.

Payment and renewal

Payment is charged to your Apple Account upon confirmation, or after the introductory trial if you confirm an eligible free-trial offer. An annual subscription renews automatically unless you cancel before renewal. Apple may charge for the next period during the 24 hours before the current period ends; cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge. The price and billing period shown in Apple’s purchase confirmation apply.

Manage or cancel

You can manage or cancel an App Store subscription in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, from Manage subscription in LECHE Settings, or on Apple’s subscription management page. Deleting LECHE does not cancel a subscription.

Use the app’s Restore Purchases option, when available, to check eligible purchases made with your Apple Account. Apple handles billing and refund requests through its support channels and Report a Problem service.

Support and privacy

For app questions, contact contact@lechespike.com. See the support page and privacy policy for more information.
